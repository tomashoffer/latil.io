The Trust Infrastructure for Digital Evidence in the AI Era 
The Threat Is Already Operational - 
AI-generated fraud has moved from edge cases to everyday risk. Manipulated, staged, replayed, or 
synthetically generated digital media is now routinely used as evidence to bypass verification 
systems, impersonate individuals, and compromise high-risk digital workflows. 
In the past two years alone, the use of AI-manipulated and fraudulent digital evidence in fraud and 
abuse incidents has grown by over 1,000%, with global losses projected to exceed $40B annually by 
2027. As generative models continue to improve, detecting manipulated content is no longer a future 
concern - it is becoming a core requirement for secure digital operations. 
Why Dtect?  
Dtect is a deep-tech company built to help organizations operate safely in this new reality. 
Founded on years of pioneering academic research and developed by a team spanning AI, computer 
vision, optics, and security-critical operations, Dtect delivers next-Gen Digital Evidence forensics 
solutions designed for environments where trust, accuracy, and reliability matter. 
At the core of Dtect’s approach is a patented dual-engine technology combining motion biometrics 
and high-frequency spectral analysis to identify Fraudulent and AI-manipulated content. The platform 
is the result of 16,000+ hours of proprietary research and is protected by five patent families, with 
thirteen granted patents, providing a robust and future-ready detection foundation.
